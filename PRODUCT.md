# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are founders, product leads, and engineering leaders at B2B SaaS startups who need senior product or software engineering on a contract basis. They are typically facing a customer-facing product, an internal tool, or a HubSpot integration stuck behind a packed roadmap, or they need senior engineering without a full-time hire. They need to understand what Hanif builds, how he works, and whether his judgment and delivery approach fit their specific engineering problem.

Secondary users are technical stakeholders and referral partners evaluating Hanif's judgment, delivery approach, and fit for a specific product-engineering problem.

## Product Purpose

The site explains what Hanif builds for B2B SaaS teams — customer-facing products, internal tools, and AI workflows — in clear language, demonstrates his engineering work through inspectable proof, and converts qualified visitors into a fit call for either a project contract or a monthly contract. It positions the practice around AI product engineering, with a clear progression from fit call to project contract or monthly contract.

Success means a visitor can quickly recognize whether Hanif fits their engineering problem, understand how he works, inspect credible evidence, and choose one appropriate next action without needing to decode a generic developer portfolio.

## Brand Personality

Calm, direct, and technically credible. The voice is source-grounded and reflective: it explains the mechanism behind a problem, avoids hype, and gives readers enough detail to make an informed decision.

The interface should evoke confidence, clarity, and practical momentum.

## Anti-references

- Generic developer or creative portfolios organized around personal taste instead of client problems.
- Employment or resume framing that makes the site read like a job application.
- AI agency hype, futuristic claims, or unsupported promises.
- Glossy generated imagery used in place of real artifacts and evidence.
- Full-site dark premium-tech styling that obscures reading and proof.
- Excess cards, dividers, and decorative containers used to manufacture hierarchy.

## Design Principles

1. **Lead with the client's engineering problem.** Help visitors recognize the feature, integration, or internal-tool bottleneck before presenting services or technology.
2. **Diagnose before prescribing.** Make the fit call to project-or-monthly contract progression explicit and avoid selling implementation before the problem is understood.
3. **Show inspectable proof.** Prefer real artifacts, outcomes, constraints, and working details over decorative claims.
4. **Maintain one clear hierarchy and action.** Every surface should make the next decision obvious, with CTA yellow reserved for the primary action.
5. **Design for client ownership.** Communicate products, tools, and integrations as understandable, maintainable systems that the client's team can operate after delivery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA as the minimum. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus states, semantic structure, reduced-motion support, useful alternative text, and text/background contrast of at least 4.5:1 for body copy. Do not rely on color alone to communicate state or priority. Keep reading measures comfortable and language clear for non-technical readers without removing the detail technical reviewers need.
