# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are founders, owners, and operations leaders in growing founder-led businesses whose recurring workflows are starting to break under growth. They need to understand where manual work, weak handoffs, scattered information, and unreliable reporting are creating operational drag, then choose a practical next step.

Secondary users are technical stakeholders and referral partners evaluating Hanif's judgment, delivery approach, and fit for a specific operational problem.

## Product Purpose

The site explains operational problems in clear language, demonstrates Hanif's technical consulting work through inspectable proof, and converts qualified visitors into a Business Systems Audit. It positions the practice around business systems engineering, with a clear progression from Audit to Build to Ongoing Technical Partnership.

Success means a visitor can quickly recognize their problem, understand how Hanif works, inspect credible evidence, and choose one appropriate next action without needing to decode a generic developer portfolio.

## Brand Personality

Calm, direct, and technically credible. The voice is operator-focused, source-grounded, and reflective: it explains the mechanism behind a problem, avoids hype, and gives readers enough detail to make an informed decision.

The interface should evoke confidence, clarity, and practical momentum.

## Anti-references

- Generic developer or creative portfolios organized around personal taste instead of client problems.
- Employment or resume framing that makes the site read like a job application.
- AI agency hype, futuristic claims, or unsupported promises.
- Glossy generated imagery used in place of real artifacts and evidence.
- Full-site dark premium-tech styling that obscures reading and proof.
- Excess cards, dividers, and decorative containers used to manufacture hierarchy.

## Design Principles

1. **Lead with the operational problem.** Help visitors recognize the workflow failure before presenting services or technology.
2. **Diagnose before prescribing.** Make the Audit to Build to Ongoing progression explicit and avoid selling implementation before the problem is understood.
3. **Show inspectable proof.** Prefer real artifacts, outcomes, constraints, and working details over decorative claims.
4. **Maintain one clear hierarchy and action.** Every surface should make the next decision obvious, with CTA yellow reserved for the primary action.
5. **Design for client ownership.** Communicate systems as understandable, maintainable tools that the client's team can operate after delivery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA as the minimum. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus states, semantic structure, reduced-motion support, useful alternative text, and text/background contrast of at least 4.5:1 for body copy. Do not rely on color alone to communicate state or priority. Keep reading measures comfortable and language clear for non-technical operators without removing the detail technical reviewers need.
