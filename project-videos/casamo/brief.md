# Project Video Brief

Project: Casamo

Video type: Product walkthrough video

Target duration: 42 seconds

Primary viewer: Nontechnical portfolio viewer or product/customer viewer

Story mode: Product walkthrough

One-sentence job: Casamo checks Airbnb and Booking.com stays against a traveler's must-haves, then returns only the choices that qualify.

Viewer takeaway: Casamo turns a noisy long-stay search into a shortlist whose evidence and unanswered questions remain visible.

Plain story:

- Problem: marketplace filters do not prove that a furnished stay will support several weeks of living and working.
- Solution: Casamo searches both marketplaces or checks exact listing links, then tests each stay against explicit requirements.
- Result: qualified choices receive equal comparison space, with support, contradictions, gaps, and host questions kept visible.

Scene arc:

1. Problem: marketplace labels alone cannot prove a long stay.
2. Intake: the traveler can search both sites or compare exact listing links.
3. Qualification: only stays that pass every must-have reach the shortlist.
4. Evidence: each requirement keeps the source behind its status.
5. Honest limits: unresolved details remain questions for the host.
6. Result: the traveler compares qualified choices without a false universal winner.

Reading-speed constraint:

- Use one short headline per scene.
- Keep each scene below nine must-read words.
- Hold every product image long enough to inspect.
- Keep implementation terms out of the on-screen copy.

Assets needed:

- Screenshot: current Casamo stay-scan intake.
- Screenshot: current qualified shortlist.
- Screenshot: current evidence-backed listing detail.
- UI clip: not needed; HyperFrames supplies motion around the static product proofs.

Privacy notes:

- Hide private accounts, customer data, payments, analytics, background jobs, tokens, local paths, and non-public listings.
- The selected screenshots are published portfolio proofs and use demonstration data.
