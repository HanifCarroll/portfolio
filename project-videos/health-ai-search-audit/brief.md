# Project Video Brief

Project: Helping a Health Services Startup Show Up in AI Search

Video type: Evidence-led audit overview

Target duration: 42 seconds

Primary viewer: Founder, website helper, or portfolio reviewer

Story mode: Public-evidence audit

One-sentence job: The review mapped where a health-services startup appeared in AI search, where it was missing, and which website fixes came next.

Viewer takeaway: This is a repeatable, evidence-backed review with clear fixes, not a claim that rankings, mentions, or sales already improved.

Plain story:

- Problem: the founder could not see when AI search tools mentioned the business or where they missed it.
- Solution: review the public website and test Spanish and English searches across four major search tools.
- Result: a private report shows the evidence, 9 website fixes, and a monthly check-in plan.

Scene arc:

1. Problem: AI search visibility was hard to see.
2. Boundary: the review used public evidence only.
3. Action: 74 search situations covered Spanish and English questions across four major tools.
4. Evidence: 33 results were checked directly in the browser.
5. Report: the findings became 9 website fixes and a monthly check-in plan.
6. Ending beat: a repeatable review, without claiming impact that has not happened.

Reading-speed constraint:

- Use one headline and one short sentence at most.
- Keep each scene at or below 18 must-read words.
- Let the public report preview carry the proof.
- Keep the final card settled for 3 seconds.

Assets needed:

- Screenshot: the public AI-search audit report preview used by the portfolio case study.
- Additional captures: none. Reframe the one public preview with different positions and contained crops.
- Native graphics: public-evidence rail, four search-tool labels, and count cards for 74, 33, and 9.

Privacy notes:

- Hide: the protected report URL, password, private client details, private report content, and any private account or website access.
- Do not claim: medical outcomes, compliance outcomes, ranking lift, more AI mentions, or sales impact.
- Safe to show: the public report preview, evidence counts already published in the case study, search-tool names, and plain review labels.
