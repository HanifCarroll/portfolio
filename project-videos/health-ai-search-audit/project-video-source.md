## Plain Story

Problem: a health-services startup wanted to know where AI search tools mentioned the business and where they missed it.

Solution: the review used public website evidence and Spanish and English searches across ChatGPT Search, Gemini, Google, and Perplexity.

Result: the findings became a private report with evidence, 9 website fixes, and a monthly check-in plan.

## Viewer Takeaway

This is a repeatable, evidence-backed review with clear fixes, not a claim that rankings, mentions, or sales already improved.
