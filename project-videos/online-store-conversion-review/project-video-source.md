## Plain Story

Problem: the store asked shoppers to choose premium botanical oils without enough help connecting unfamiliar names to their needs.

Solution: the review followed the public path from homepage to skin assessment, product list, and product page on desktop and mobile.

Result: five findings became seven fixes in order, with a result to watch after each change.

## Viewer Takeaway

The owner can see where shoppers get stuck, which fixes come first, and what to measure after changes.

## Language Rules

Say:

- Shopping path
- Skin assessment
- Product fit
- Sold-out products
- Product questions
- Mobile speed
- Five findings
- Seven fixes
- Result to watch

Do not claim:

- A sales increase
- A conversion increase
- A health or skincare outcome
- A fix the owner has already implemented
- Private store data that was not available for the review
