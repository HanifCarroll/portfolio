# Expanded production prompt: Project Video AI Workflow

## Style block

Create a 39.5-second square LinkedIn proof reel that presents the portfolio video workflow as an editorial evidence cabinet becoming a production line. The visual system follows `frame.md` exactly: canvas `#F9F9F7`, paper `#FFFCF6`, ink `#171512`, body `#3A352F`, muted `#696158`, system blue `#0066FF`, verified green `#1D6F58`, and judgment amber `#C7862F`. Use EB Garamond 700 for human statements, Inter 400/700 for explanatory copy, and IBM Plex Mono 700 for fields, labels, and status.

The conceptual tension is human judgment versus mechanical reliability. Serif statements should feel considered and editorial. Monospaced labels and system rails should feel exact, repeatable, and inspectable.

## Rhythm

`HOLD-scatter-assemble-SLOW SPLIT-rapid proof-snap sync-hold`

## Global rules

- Work at 1080x1080 with at least 92px safe margins.
- Keep project videos intact in a 16:9 proof window; use `object-fit: contain` and never crop their UI.
- Use a warm grid, edge rails, registration corners, metadata labels, and rules in every scene.
- Each hero frame has a background, midground, and foreground layer with roughly eight visible elements.
- Use explicit, seek-safe `fromTo()` entrances. Do not animate `display`, dimensions, or layout offsets.
- Full-frame two-block cover wipes hide every major scene swap. Scene 05 uses fast hard cuts because it is a rapid proof list.
- No exit animation on scenes 01-06; the transition is the exit. Scene 07 may gently resolve.
- All project clips remain silent. No audio track is mounted.

## Scene 01 - One system - 0.0-4.0s

### Concept

The viewer arrives inside a tidy evidence cabinet already in motion. Six public project artifacts begin near the center and assemble into an ordered field while the outcome lands immediately: 21 case studies now share one video system.

### Mood

Editorial archive meets precise production desk. Confident and practical, not futuristic.

### Depth layers

- BG: warm canvas; subtle grid drifting four pixels; oversized `01`; top and bottom metadata rails.
- MG: six framed project posters expanding from the center into a two-column proof field.
- FG: statement panel, `PORTFOLIO VIDEO WORKFLOW` label, registration corners, blue count chip, and thin rule.

### Choreography

- Proof cards EXPAND from a partially spread center using index-derived offsets and a `power3.out` settle.
- The `21` count LOCKS IN first; the statement SLIDES up next; metadata DRAWS across last.
- Cards hold with one bounded two-pixel counter-phase drift.

### Transition out

Two full-frame blocks in `#0066FF` and `#FFFCF6` WIPE left-to-right over 0.64s, covering the scene swap at 4.0s.

## Recurring motifs

- `video.json` field labels and source-backed proof windows.
- Scene numbers in IBM Plex Mono at the upper-left edge.
- Registration corners around evidence surfaces.
- A blue system line that becomes ordered as the story progresses.
- Green appears only after a state is verified; amber appears only where judgment is exercised.

## Negative prompt

No neon tech aesthetic, gradient text, floating decorative blobs, cropped UI, fake metrics, generic robot/brain imagery, framework logos, code as the main message, or claims of full automation. Do not let small metadata compete with the primary statement or cover public proof.
