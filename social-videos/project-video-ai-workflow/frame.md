# Project Video AI Workflow Frame

## Concept

An editorial evidence cabinet becomes a production line: public project proof stays visually rich while a shared system routes every case study through the same reliable production process.

## Brand contract

- Canvas: `#F9F9F7`; paper surfaces: `#FFFCF6`; primary ink: `#171512`; body: `#3A352F`; muted: `#696158`.
- Blue `#0066FF` marks the shared system. Green `#1D6F58` is reserved for verified or QA states. Amber `#C7862F` marks judgment or decisions.
- EB Garamond 700 carries human statements. Inter 400/700 carries explanatory copy. IBM Plex Mono 700 carries labels, counts, fields, and status.
- Headline scale is `88-118px`; body is `32-40px`; labels are `24-28px`.
- Keep at least `92px` safe margins. Public 16:9 project media stays intact inside framed proof windows and is never center-cropped.

## Visual language

Every scene uses three layers:

1. Background: warm canvas, subtle editorial grid, edge rails, and one oversized index mark.
2. Midground: a proof window, manifest card, comparison pair, or system diagram.
3. Foreground: scene number, family/status labels, thin rules, registration corners, and compact metadata.

The frame should feel produced at rest: two focal points and roughly eight visible elements without covering the proof surface.

## Motion

- Medium-energy editorial pacing with explicit `fromTo()` entrances.
- The main rhythm is `HOLD-scatter-assemble-SLOW SPLIT-rapid proof-snap sync-hold`.
- Full-frame blue/paper cover wipes hide scene swaps. Rapid project examples use deliberate hard cuts.
- Use center-outward assembly for the opening evidence cabinet, opposing split cards for AI/software responsibilities, and synchronized counters for final output proof.
- One finite ambient motion per scene: grid drift, rule pulse, card settle, or proof-window scale settle.
- The final scene settles within 0.5 seconds and holds for at least three seconds.

## Constraints

- Silent-first: no narration, music, or sound effects.
- Do not imply GPT renders pixels. AI judgment shapes the manifest; HyperFrames and the shared software render deterministically.
- Do not call the workflow fully automated. Human visual and privacy approval remains mandatory.
- Do not claim all 21 videos were rebuilt. Nineteen previous compositions were migrated; two case studies received their first videos.
- Shared layout and motion are centralized. Project-specific palettes, evidence, copy, and privacy rules remain in each manifest.
- No generic neon-on-dark treatment, gradient text, decorative blobs, or cropped project footage.
