<CaseSection title="The starting point">

The founders of a new beauty brand had product ideas, but making the products and holding inventory meant spending money up front. They wanted to know whether people would buy before they built a full store.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="What I built" media>

I built a storefront that works like a normal online shop until the last step. When someone clicks to buy, it tells them the product will be available soon and asks for an email instead of payment, so the founders could count buying attempts per product without shipping anything.

<ImagePlate image={casaElariaFeature} alt="Casa Elaria fake-door e-commerce storefront." />

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="What happened">

Visitors clicked a buy button 38 times, and 3 of them left their email, two for the shower oil and one for the bar soap. All three signups came in September 2025 through Instagram and Facebook.

</CaseSection>
