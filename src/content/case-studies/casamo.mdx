<CaseSection title="A long stay needs more than marketplace filters">
  Remote workers booking a furnished place for several weeks need more than a Wi-Fi or workspace tag. They need to know what the photos, listing details, amenities, and recent reviews can support before they commit money and travel plans.

Casamo checks Airbnb and Booking.com listings against the traveler's must-haves, then returns a shortlist of places that passed. Anything the source material cannot establish stays visible as a question for the host.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="Make the must-haves explicit before search">
  <DecisionBlock title="Return qualified choices instead of filling a quota.">
    A listing stays off the shortlist when it fails a required priority. When enough places qualify, the report returns 6–10 choices and gives each one equal space rather than declaring a universal winner.
  </DecisionBlock>

<DecisionBlock title="Show what supports each claim.">
  The report marks a requirement as supported, needing confirmation, contradicted, or not evaluated.
  It also links that status to the photo, amenity, listing detail, or review behind it.
</DecisionBlock>

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="From trip brief to saved report">
  <DecisionBlock title="Search the market or compare exact links.">
    A traveler can enter a destination and dates or paste 2–10 Airbnb or Booking.com links. Both paths use the same requirements and listing checks.
  </DecisionBlock>

<DecisionBlock title="Keep longer scans out of the request path.">
  The scan runs as background work, preserves its source material, saves the finished report, and
  restores the scan credit when a run fails or is unusable.
</DecisionBlock>

<DecisionBlock title="Support both one-time and repeat use.">
  The beta includes one free eligible scan. One-time and monthly plans add clear allowances for
  travelers who need more searches.
</DecisionBlock>

</CaseSection>
