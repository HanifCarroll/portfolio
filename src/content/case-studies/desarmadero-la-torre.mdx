<CaseSection title="I built a searchable customer-facing parts catalog">
  I built a searchable customer-facing parts catalog so buyers could check stock before calling.

    Desarmadero La Torre had a large, changing auto-parts inventory, but customers had to call or visit to find out what was available. I turned that phone-heavy process into a searchable digital stockroom that customers could browse before contacting the team.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="Staff answered the same stock questions all day">
  The sales workflow depended on staff repeatedly answering the same availability questions.

    That created friction on both sides: customers had no way to inspect stock, and staff had to spend phone time qualifying basic requests.

    A useful change had to make inventory visible without forcing the staff into a heavy replacement system they would struggle to maintain.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="I made stock searchable before changing the internal system">
