<CaseSection title="The starting point">

Josh owns JIEC Labs, an online store on Shopify that sells botanical oils for skin and hair. He wanted more of his visitors to buy. The oils have a strong story, but shoppers had to understand unfamiliar ingredient names before they could choose one.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="What I found">

I followed the path a shopper takes, from the homepage to the skin assessment, the product list, and a product page, on desktop and mobile. Five problems stood out:

- **The skin assessment was a dead end.** The homepage promoted it, but the page showed no questions and pointed to no product.
- **Products were hard to choose between.** They were listed by botanical name, not by the concern a shopper had, like dryness or sensitive skin.
- **Most of the shelf was sold out.** Six of the 11 products were out of stock.
- **Product pages asked for the sale too early.** The buy button came before who the product was for and how to use it.
- **Mobile was slow.** On Google's speed test, a product page scored 90 on desktop but 60 on mobile.

I sent him a short report with the fixes in order. He asked about pricing the same day.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="What I built">

Before quoting, I built a clickable prototype of a new assessment, an ingredient guide, and a blog in his store's style, and changed it twice from his notes. The full set came to $2,000. His budget was $750, so we cut it to the changes that mattered most for buying:

- **A Personal Skin & Hair Assessment.** It asks where and why someone wants an oil, recommends from all 11 products, and skips anything out of stock.
- **"Best for" filters and summaries.** Shoppers can filter by concerns like dryness, glow, or hair care, and every product card says who it's for.
- **Clearer product pages.** Each one groups who it's best for, its benefits, and how to use it, and Josh can edit all of it in Shopify.
- **Help when something is sold out.** Sold-out product pages show available alternatives.
- **Better search.** Search finds products by name, by concern, and by the terms from the assessment.

I built it all in an unpublished theme so the live store didn't change until he approved it. He reviewed each part, and I made his changes before launch.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="What happened">

The new theme went live on August 7, after I checked it in Chrome and Safari on desktop and mobile. Ten days later Josh said everything was working well.

</CaseSection>
