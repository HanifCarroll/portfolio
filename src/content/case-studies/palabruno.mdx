<CaseSection title="An early prototype needed a first buyer">
  Palabruno began with an early prototype for a Spanish reader. Learners could tap a word for translation, grammar, and pronunciation help without leaving the text. I helped take it through launch on iOS, Android, and web.

    I worked directly with the founder to decide who would pay first, scope the learner and teacher workflows, add payments, complete the store launches, and prepare the handoff.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="I treated tutors as the first buyer to test">
  <DecisionBlock title="Sell to tutors first.">
    Solo learners could use the reader, but tutors already needed to create readings and send them to students. That made tutors the first buyer worth testing.
    </DecisionBlock>

    <DecisionBlock title="Keep learner and teacher work separate.">
      I narrowed the product into a mobile reader for students and a web workspace for teachers. That let each audience get the workflow it needed without forcing every feature into the same interface.

      The mobile product centered on reading, saved texts, translation support, and Premium upgrades. The teacher workspace handled class management, custom readings, sharing, and paid teacher accounts.
    </DecisionBlock>

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="Students read on mobile while teachers prepare lessons on the web">
  The shipped product connected three surfaces: the mobile reader, the teacher web workspace, and the public launch site.

    On mobile, learners can read Spanish content, tap words for support, save useful readings, and upgrade to Premium through the app stores.

    On web, teachers can create custom readings from lesson notes, manage students and groups, share material, track recent shares, and buy the Teacher plan through Stripe.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="Payments, store launches, and handoff were part of the MVP">
  <DecisionBlock title="Use mobile billing for Premium and web billing for Teacher.">
    RevenueCat handles Premium subscriptions through Apple and Google. Stripe handles Teacher payments on the web, where tutors use the workspace.
    </DecisionBlock>

    <DecisionBlock title="Leave the founder with a product he could run.">
      I prepared the legal pages, store listings, screenshots, release docs, public copy, and handoff notes. The founder could operate the product and keep testing the tutor-first offer after the engagement.
    </DecisionBlock>

</CaseSection>
