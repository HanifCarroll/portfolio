<CaseSection title="From Instagram fragments to a working pitch">
  RIOwell was preparing to open a neighborhood recovery and wellness club in Núñez. The business had a new Instagram account full of useful material, but no website that brought its offer together.

I treated the website as the pitch. I researched the public source material, shaped the product story, designed the experience, and shipped a responsive Spanish-first preview before contacting the business.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="A neighborhood club needed one clear story">
  The club combined small-group movement classes, sauna, cryotherapy, massage, recovery equipment, memberships, and a social patio. On Instagram, those ideas were spread across posts, highlight slides, and a narrated facility tour.

A prospective member still needed clear answers: what is this place, which experience fits me, and how do I start?

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="The public source material set the boundaries">
  <DecisionBlock title="Build from the real business, not a generic wellness template.">
    I captured and reviewed 50 public media files, preserved the club's Spanish copy, and organized the service catalog, membership structure, location details, brand language, and gaps in the available information.
  </DecisionBlock>

<DecisionBlock title="Treat missing information as a product constraint.">
  Prices and class schedules were not public. Instead of filling the page with estimates, the final
  concept explains the offer and routes those questions to WhatsApp.
</DecisionBlock>

  <DecisionBlock title="Use stronger imagery without inventing business claims.">
    The existing social photography was not strong enough to carry a premium web experience. I developed a calm editorial image direction while keeping every statement about services, memberships, and the space tied to the public source material.
  </DecisionBlock>
</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="A calm visual system for a premium offer" media>
  The final direction is warm, restrained, and softly premium. Large editorial typography and generous space create calm. The hero gives visitors one clear promise and one action.

  <ImagePlate image={heroScreen} alt="RIOwell website hero with a warm wellness photograph, Spanish headline, navigation, and WhatsApp action." />
</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="The page follows how a member decides">
  <DecisionBlock title="Organize the story around move, recover, and belong.">
    The page introduces three paths before expanding into movement classes, recovery services, the club experience, memberships, and the visit section. That structure follows the founder's description of the space.
  </DecisionBlock>

<DecisionBlock title="Keep one primary conversion path.">
  Early versions repeated WhatsApp controls too often. The final pass reduced them to deliberate
  points in the header, hero, memberships, and closing section.
</DecisionBlock>

  <DecisionBlock title="Make the neighborhood location tangible.">
    The visit section includes the real address, opening hours, transport context, and a lazy-loaded Google Maps embed so the site answers practical questions before the conversation starts.
  </DecisionBlock>
</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="Movement feels guided, not performance-heavy" media>
  Movement needed to feel approachable rather than performance-heavy. The section uses the club's five activity types, small-group positioning, and close-guidance promise within a quiet editorial layout.

  <ImagePlate image={movementScreen} alt="RIOwell movement section with an editorial heading and Pilates photography." />
</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="Recovery shifts the atmosphere, not the system" media>
  Recovery shifts into a cooler sage field and warmer, lower-light imagery. The change in atmosphere separates restoration from training while keeping both parts of one coherent brand system.

  <ImagePlate image={recoveryScreen} alt="RIOwell recovery section with a sauna scene in a muted sage layout." />
</CaseSection>
