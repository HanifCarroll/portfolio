<CaseSection title="The starting point">

A coach who works with clients one to one came to me for help. She had finished online courses, coaching packages, a mailing list, and an audience on social media. Nothing connected them. Her courses had never been launched, and new clients came only by word of mouth.

On our call she asked for "a step by step" and said she wanted "to make it so easy to pay." She also didn't want more offers to cost her the freedom she has now.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="What I found">

I followed her public links the way a new visitor would. The main button opened a call about long-term coaching, so someone who wanted help with one thing had no smaller step to take, and no way to pay for one.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="What I delivered">

I designed one path on the course platform she already paid for. Her posts and events point to a short free practice. Signing up starts a few welcome emails, and those lead to a new single paid session. The session leads to coaching for anyone who wants ongoing support, and the courses sit beside that path for people who prefer to go at their own pace.

A few decisions shaped it:

- **The session comes first.** She said people in her network would buy a single session before anything else, so it became the main offer.
- **One free gift.** A short practice matches what she already teaches.
- **About an hour a week.** One teaching post replaces a regular post instead of adding to her load.

I sent it as a Google Doc with 15 setup steps in order. One step has her test every signup on her phone before sharing it, so no one lands on a broken link. The doc also explains how to hand the setup to an AI assistant, and she did the setup herself with one.

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection title="What happened">

A week later her courses were on sale and the single session was open for booking.

</CaseSection>
