## What it is

An automation tool checks one app for an event and then takes a step in another app. For example: when someone fills in the contact form on your website, add a row to your spreadsheet and send you a WhatsApp message. You set this up once by choosing the apps and the steps, and the tool runs it each time the event happens.

## When you need it

- You copy information between apps by hand, such as orders from your online store into a spreadsheet.
- You update the same spreadsheet by hand at the end of each day.
- You send the same email after each booking or purchase.
- You've lost a sale or given a refund because someone retyped information wrong.

An automation is worth it when the same steps happen many times a week and the steps don't change.

## Popular choices

- **Zapier**: Connects to many common business apps and suits a first automation.
- **Make**: Shows each automation as a visual map of steps. It fits automations with several branches.
- **n8n**: Can run on your own server instead of n8n's. It fits you if someone technical can keep that server running.
- **Built-in automations**: Many business apps include simple automations of their own. Check what your apps already do before you pay for another tool.

## What to watch for

- **Price per task.** These tools often charge for each step they run, so you may need a higher plan when an automation runs many times a day.
- **Silent failures.** If an app changes or a password expires, an automation can stop, and you won't see a message unless you set one up. Turn on error emails and read them.
- **Who can change it.** Keep a short note of what each automation does, so someone else can fix it if the person who built it leaves.
