## What it is

Off-the-shelf software is an app anyone can sign up for, such as a booking or accounting app. Custom software is a tool a developer builds for one business, such as an order form that matches your menu or a page that combines sales from two shops. Custom software can also be small: a connection between two apps, a script that fills in a spreadsheet, or one page added to your website.

## When you need it

- You've tried several apps, and each one covers only part of how you work.
- You pay for three or four apps and copy information between them every day.
- Your way of working is what makes the business different, and you would have to change it to fit a general app.
- You need a report or a page that no app offers.

If a common app covers most of what you need, start with it. It is usually cheaper and faster than custom software.

## Popular choices

- **An app plus settings**: You can fix many problems by changing an app's settings, without new software.
- **An app plus automations**: Connect the apps you have with an automation tool or their APIs.
- **A small custom tool**: A form, page, or report built for your business, often on top of the apps you already use.
- **A custom app**: A larger build, such as a customer portal or an internal system. This costs the most and needs someone to keep it updated.

## What to watch for

- **Who maintains it.** Custom software needs updates and fixes. Agree on who does this and how you pay for it.
- **Who owns the code and accounts.** Make sure the code, hosting, and domain are in the business's name.
- **Start small.** Build the smallest version that solves the main problem, use it, and then decide what to add.
