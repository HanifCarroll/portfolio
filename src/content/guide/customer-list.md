## What it is

A CRM (customer relationship management) app is a shared list of your customers and people who asked about your work. For each person it stores their contact details, notes from past conversations, quotes you sent, and the next step, such as "send the quote on Friday". Most CRMs can send you a reminder when a next step is due.

## When you need it

- You keep customer details in your phone, a notebook, and your email, and you search all three to find one person.
- People ask for a quote, and nobody on your team follows up after you send it.
- Someone on your team leaves, and you lose the customer notes they kept on their own phone.
- Your customer spreadsheet has grown to many tabs, and two people edit it at once.

If you have a few regular customers and you remember them all, a spreadsheet is enough. A CRM is worth it when you can no longer remember every next step.

## Popular choices

- **HubSpot**: A CRM with a contact list and deals, plus paid plans that add email marketing and more. The free plan covers up to 2 users. The Starter plan costs $20 per user a month, with a discount for new customers that starts at $7 per user a month billed yearly or $10 billed monthly. Tax is extra.
- **Pipedrive**: Shows each quote or deal as a card that moves through stages. It fits a business that sends many quotes. There is no free plan, only a 14-day trial. The Lite plan costs $14 per user a month billed yearly.
- **Zoho CRM**: A CRM with plans for small teams. The free plan covers up to 3 users and allows 10 exports. The Standard plan costs $14 per user a month billed yearly, or $20 billed monthly. Local taxes are extra.
- **A shared spreadsheet**: Google Sheets or Excel with one row per customer. It costs nothing extra if you already use Google Sheets or Excel, and fits a small team that updates it every day.

Prices as of November 2026.

## What to watch for

- **Price per user.** Most CRMs charge for each person who signs in, so the monthly cost grows with the team.
- **Free plans with limits.** Free plans often cap contacts or features, and the next plan up can cost much more.
- **Nobody updates it.** A CRM is useful only if your team adds each call and quote. Choose the simplest one your team will use.
- **Exporting your customers.** Check that you can download every contact and note as a spreadsheet.
