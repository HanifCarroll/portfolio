## What it is

An electronic signature, or e-signature, is a way to sign a document on a screen instead of on paper. An e-signature app sends the document to each person who needs to sign, records who signed and when, and saves a signed copy for everyone.

## When you need it

- You print contracts, sign them, scan them, and send them back.
- A client agrees to a quote by message, and you have no signed record.
- You wait days for a signature because the client has no printer.

## Popular choices

- **DocuSign**: An e-signature service. There is no free plan. The Personal plan, for one user, costs $11 a month on a yearly commitment of $132 and includes 5 envelopes a month. An envelope is one set of documents you send for signing.
- **Dropbox Sign**: E-signatures from the same company as Dropbox. The free plan includes 3 signature requests a month. The Essentials plan costs $15 a month billed yearly, or $20 billed monthly.
- **Adobe Acrobat Sign**: E-signatures inside Adobe's PDF tools. Acrobat Standard, the cheapest individual plan that includes e-signatures, costs $14.99 a month on a yearly plan billed monthly.
- **PandaDoc**: Quotes and proposals with e-signatures built in. The free plan includes 5 documents a month with up to 2 recipients each. The Starter plan costs $19 per user a month billed yearly, or $35 billed monthly.

Prices as of November 2026.

## What to watch for

- **Legal validity.** Countries treat electronic signatures differently. In Argentina, the law separates a digital signature (firma digital) from an electronic signature (firma electrónica), so ask your lawyer or accountant which one a document needs.
- **Price by sender.** Many services charge for each person who sends documents, and some limit how many you can send each month.
- **Templates.** Save your usual contract as a template, so you fill in only the client's details each time.
- **Keeping signed copies.** Download signed documents to your own files as well as keeping them in the app.
