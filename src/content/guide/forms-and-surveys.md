## What it is

An online form is a page with questions that customers fill in on a phone or computer. A form tool saves every answer in one table, usually a spreadsheet, and can send you an email for each new answer. You can use forms for intake, quote requests, orders, sign-ups, and feedback.

## When you need it

- You collect customer details on paper forms and type them in later.
- You ask each new client the same questions by message.
- You want feedback from customers and don't have a way to collect it.

## Popular choices

- **Google Forms**: Simple forms that save answers to Google Sheets.
- **Microsoft Forms**: Simple forms that save answers to Excel.
- **Tally**: Forms that look like a plain document page.
- **Jotform**: Many templates, payment fields, and signatures.
- **Typeform**: Forms that show one question at a time.

## What to watch for

- **Personal data.** Ask only for what you need, tell people why, and keep sensitive details, such as health information, in tools meant for them.
- **Answer limits.** Some form tools limit answers each month on cheaper plans.
- **Where answers go.** Decide who reads new answers and how soon, so nobody leaves a request unread in a spreadsheet.
