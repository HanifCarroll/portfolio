## What it is

A Google Business Profile is the box that appears in Google Search and Google Maps when someone searches for your business or for businesses like yours nearby. It shows your address, opening hours, phone number, website, photos, and customer reviews. You claim it and edit it from a Google account.

## When you need it

- People search for your business and can't find your hours, address, or phone number.
- Google shows old hours or a wrong phone number, and you don't know how to change them.
- Customers leave reviews, and nobody from the business replies.
- You serve a local area, and people near you search on their phones before they visit.

## Popular choices

- **Google Business Profile**: The listing in Google Search and Maps. Google says you can manage it at no charge with a Google account. Ads on Maps and Search are a separate, paid option.
- **Apple Business Connect**: The same kind of listing for Apple Maps.
- **Bing Places for Business**: The listing for Bing search and maps. Microsoft says listing your business is free.

Prices as of November 2026.

## What to watch for

- **Who manages the listing.** Make the business's own Google account the owner and add staff as managers, so you keep the listing if someone leaves.
- **Hours on holidays.** Update special hours before holidays, so customers don't arrive while you're closed.
- **Replying to reviews.** Reply to reviews, both good and bad, in a calm and short way. Other customers read the replies.
- **Fake reviews.** Don't buy reviews or offer rewards for good ones. Google's rules don't allow fake reviews.
