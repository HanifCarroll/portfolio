## What it is

Inventory software keeps a count of each product you have. When you sell something, the count goes down, and when you restock, it goes up. Order tracking keeps a list of each order with its status, such as new, paid, packed, or delivered. Many POS apps and store builders include both.

## When you need it

- You track orders and bookings on paper or in WhatsApp.
- You find out a product is sold out when a customer asks for it.
- You sell in a shop and online, and you sell the same last item twice.
- You count stock by hand before each order to a supplier.

If you sell a few products and one person handles all orders, a shared spreadsheet may be enough.

## Popular choices

- **Your POS or store builder**: Square, Shopify, and Tiendanube include stock counts. Check what yours does before you add another app. In the U.S., Square's free plan costs $0 a month per location and includes an item list that tracks stock, and Square Plus costs $49 a month per location. Shopify's Basic plan includes inventory tracking for $29 a month billed yearly, or $39 billed monthly.
- **Zoho Inventory**: Inventory and orders for businesses that sell in several places. The free plan includes 50 orders, 1 user, and 1 location. The Standard plan costs $29 a month per organization billed yearly, or $39 billed monthly, for 500 orders a month, 3 users, and 2 locations.
- **Sortly**: A simple app for counting items. The free plan covers 100 unique items, meaning different products rather than units in stock, and 1 user. The Advanced plan costs $49 a month for 500 unique items and 2 users.
- **A shared spreadsheet**: Google Sheets or Excel with one row per product.

Prices as of November 2026.

## What to watch for

- **One count, not two.** If your shop and website each keep their own count, they will differ. Use one app as the source of the count, or connect them.
- **Counting by hand.** Software shows the count you entered. Do a physical count from time to time and correct it.
- **Price by location or order volume.** Some apps charge more for more locations or more orders.
