## What it is

An invoicing app creates and sends invoices and records which ones are paid. An accounting app goes further: it records every sale and expense, connects to your bank account, and produces the reports your accountant uses for taxes. Many apps do both.

## When you need it

- You write invoices by hand or in a Word file.
- You check your bank account to see which customers paid.
- You sort a year of receipts for your accountant at the end of the year.
- You can't say how much you earned last month without adding it up.

## Popular choices

- **QuickBooks Online**: Accounting for small businesses, from Intuit.
- **Xero**: Accounting with bank connections and invoicing, used in many countries.
- **Zoho Books**: Accounting and invoicing from the same company as Zoho CRM.
- **ARCA (formerly AFIP)**: In Argentina, electronic invoices go through ARCA. You can issue them from ARCA's own website or from an accounting app that connects to it.

## What to watch for

- **Ask your accountant first.** Many accountants work with one or two apps. Choosing one they know saves you both time.
- **Tax rules where you are.** Invoicing rules differ by country. Check that the app can produce invoices that are valid where you sell.
- **Bank connections.** Check that the app can connect to your bank, or that you can import your bank statements.
- **Exporting your records.** Check that you can download your invoices and records, because you may need to keep them for years.
