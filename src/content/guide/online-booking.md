## What it is

Online booking software shows your free times on a web page. A client picks a time, enters their name and phone number, and the booking appears on your calendar. Most booking apps also send the client a confirmation and a reminder by email or text message before the appointment.

## When you need it

- You take appointments by phone or by message, and each one takes a few messages back and forth to agree on a time.
- Clients forget appointments, and you lose the income from that time.
- You keep the schedule on paper or in your head, so only you can answer "Are you free on Thursday?"
- Several people on your team take bookings, and two of them sometimes book the same slot.

If you take a handful of appointments a week and nobody misses one, a paper diary or a shared calendar may be enough.

## Popular choices

- **Calendly**: Built for booking meetings and calls. It fits a consultant, coach, or anyone who meets clients one at a time.
- **Square Appointments**: Booking and card payments from the same company. It fits a salon, barber, or studio that also takes payment at the front desk.
- **Fresha**: Booking made for salons, barbers, and spas.
- **Google Calendar appointment schedules**: A booking page built into Google Calendar. It fits you if you already keep your schedule in Google Calendar and need a simple page.

## What to watch for

- **Price per staff member.** Many booking apps charge for each person who takes bookings, so the monthly cost grows as you hire.
- **Fees on payments and deposits.** If clients pay a deposit when they book, check the fee on each payment as well as the monthly price.
- **Reminders by text message.** Some apps charge extra for text-message reminders. Check before you count on them.
- **Taking your client list with you.** Check that you can download your clients and appointment history as a spreadsheet before you add years of records.
