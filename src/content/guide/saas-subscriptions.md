## What it is

SaaS stands for software as a service. It means software you use in a browser or app and pay for with a subscription, monthly or yearly, instead of buying it once. The company updates it, stores your information, and keeps it running. Most business apps today, from booking to accounting, are SaaS.

## When you need it

- You pay for several apps and don't know the total each month.
- An app's price went up and you didn't notice until the card was charged.
- You pay for apps nobody on the team uses anymore.

## Popular choices

- **Per user**: You pay for each person who signs in. Common in CRMs, chat, and email.
- **Per location or account**: You pay for each shop, calendar, or social account.
- **By usage**: You pay for what you use, such as tasks, messages, or contacts.
- **Free plans**: Many apps have a free plan with limits, and you move to a paid plan when you reach them.

## What to watch for

- **Yearly versus monthly.** Yearly billing is often cheaper per month, but you pay in advance and may not get a refund if you stop.
- **Costs that grow.** Per-user and per-contact prices go up as the business grows. Check the price at the size you expect next year.
- **A list of subscriptions.** Keep one list with each app, its price, its renewal date, and who uses it. Review it a few times a year and cancel what you don't use.
- **Your data when you cancel.** Export your records before you cancel. See the entry on your data.
