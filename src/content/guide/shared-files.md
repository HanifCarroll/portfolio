## What it is

Shared file storage keeps your files on a company's servers instead of only on one computer or phone. Everyone on your team opens the same folder from any device, and two people can edit the same document instead of emailing copies to each other.

## When you need it

- Your files are spread across phones, laptops, and email.
- You email documents to yourself to move them between devices.
- Two people edit different copies of the same price list, and nobody knows which one is current.
- A staff member leaves, and you lose the files that were only on their laptop.

## Popular choices

- **Google Drive**: File storage with Docs and Sheets, part of Google Workspace. Business Starter includes 30 GB of storage per user and costs $7 per user a month on a yearly plan, or $8.40 on a flexible monthly plan.
- **Microsoft OneDrive and SharePoint**: File storage with Word and Excel, part of Microsoft 365. Business Basic includes 1 TB of storage per user and costs $7 per user a month paid yearly, or $8.40 with a monthly subscription.
- **Dropbox**: File storage that works with both Google and Microsoft apps. The free Basic plan includes 2 GB. Business plans start with Standard, and Dropbox's pages show more than one price for it, so check the price for your team size and country.

Prices as of November 2026.

## What to watch for

- **A business account, not a personal one.** Keep business files in an account the business owns, and share folders with staff instead of letting each person keep their own.
- **Sharing links.** A link set to "anyone with the link" opens the file for anyone who gets the link. Share with named people for private files.
- **Sync is not a backup.** If someone deletes a file in a synced folder, the file is deleted on every device. Keep a separate backup.
