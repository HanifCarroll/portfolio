## What it is

A social media scheduling app lets you write posts for Instagram, Facebook, and other networks ahead of time and pick the day and hour to publish each one. You can write a week of posts at one time, and the app publishes each one at the time you set.

## When you need it

- You post only when you remember, and you go weeks without posting.
- You stop work in the middle of a busy day to write a post.
- You post the same news on several networks, one at a time.

If you post once or twice a week on one network, the network's own scheduling feature may be enough.

## Popular choices

- **Meta Business Suite**: Meta's own app for scheduling posts on Facebook and Instagram.
- **Buffer**: A simple scheduler for several networks.
- **Later**: A scheduler built around Instagram and visual posts.
- **Hootsuite**: A scheduler with tools for larger teams.

## What to watch for

- **Price by account and user.** Many schedulers charge more for each social account or team member.
- **Post types.** Check that the scheduler can publish the kinds of posts you make, such as Stories or Reels, before you pay for it.
- **Who owns the accounts.** Connect the business's own social accounts, not a staff member's personal one.
