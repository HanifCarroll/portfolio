## What it is

A staff scheduling app is where you build the weekly staff schedule. Each person sees their shifts on their phone, asks for time off, and offers shifts to swap, and you approve the changes. Many of these apps also record when people clock in and out.

## When you need it

- You make the staff schedule on paper or in a spreadsheet and send a photo of it to a group chat.
- You spend time each week on shift swaps by message.
- Staff miss shifts because they saw an older version of the schedule.
- You add up hours for pay by hand.

## Popular choices

- **Homebase**: Scheduling and time clocks for shops and restaurants.
- **When I Work**: Scheduling, shift swaps, and time clocks.
- **Deputy**: Scheduling and time tracking for teams in several locations.
- **7shifts**: Scheduling made for restaurants.

## What to watch for

- **Labor rules.** Rules on breaks, overtime, and rest between shifts differ by country. Check them yourself instead of relying on the app's defaults.
- **Price per person.** Most apps charge for each scheduled person or each location.
- **Connection to payroll.** If you use a payroll service, check that the app can send hours to it, or export them as a spreadsheet.
