## What it is

A staff scheduling app is where you build the weekly staff schedule. Each person sees their shifts on their phone, asks for time off, and offers shifts to swap, and you approve the changes. Many of these apps also record when people clock in and out.

## When you need it

- You make the staff schedule on paper or in a spreadsheet and send a photo of it to a group chat.
- You spend time each week on shift swaps by message.
- Staff miss shifts because they saw an older version of the schedule.
- You add up hours for pay by hand.

## Popular choices

- **Homebase**: Scheduling and time clocks for shops and restaurants. The free Basic plan covers 1 location and up to 10 employees. The Essentials plan costs $24 a month per location billed yearly, or $30 billed monthly, with unlimited employees.
- **When I Work**: Scheduling, shift swaps, and time clocks. The pricing page lists a free trial and no free plan. The Essentials plan costs $2.50 per user a month. The pricing page says plans start at $2.50 per user a month for one location, and $5 for several locations.
- **Deputy**: Scheduling and time tracking for teams in several locations. The pricing page lists a free trial and no free plan. The Lite plan costs $5 per user a month, with a minimum of $30 a month on monthly plans.
- **7shifts**: Scheduling made for restaurants. The free Comp plan covers 1 location and up to 15 employees. Paid plans start with Essentials, priced per location each month.

Prices as of November 2026.

## What to watch for

- **Labor rules.** Rules on breaks, overtime, and rest between shifts differ by country. Check them yourself instead of relying on the app's defaults.
- **Price per person.** Most apps charge for each scheduled person or each location.
- **Connection to payroll.** If you use a payroll service, check that the app can send hours to it, or export them as a spreadsheet.
