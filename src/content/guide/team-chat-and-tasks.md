## What it is

A team chat app is like WhatsApp for work, with separate channels for topics such as orders or deliveries. A task app is a shared list where each task has a person, a due date, and a status. Some apps include both.

## When you need it

- You send tasks to your team in WhatsApp and can't tell which ones are done.
- Work messages and personal messages are mixed in the same groups.
- A task depends on one person remembering it.
- New staff ask where to find things that someone explained in a chat months ago.

## Popular choices

- **Slack**: Team chat with channels and connections to many other apps.
- **Microsoft Teams**: Team chat and video calls, part of Microsoft 365.
- **Google Chat**: Team chat, part of Google Workspace.
- **Trello**: Tasks shown as cards on a board, with one column for each stage.
- **Asana**: Task lists and projects for teams.

## What to watch for

- **One more app.** A team uses a new app only if it's simpler than the group chat they have. Start with one channel or one board and add more later.
- **Price per user.** Most of these apps charge for each person, so the cost grows with the team.
- **Phones for staff.** Decide whether staff use their own phones for work apps, and remove them from the workspace when they leave.
