## What it is

A team chat app is like WhatsApp for work, with separate channels for topics such as orders or deliveries. A task app is a shared list where each task has a person, a due date, and a status. Some apps include both.

## When you need it

- You send tasks to your team in WhatsApp and can't tell which ones are done.
- Work messages and personal messages are mixed in the same groups.
- A task depends on one person remembering it.
- New staff ask where to find things that someone explained in a chat months ago.

## Popular choices

- **Slack**: Team chat with channels and connections to many other apps. On the free plan, you can see messages and files from the last 90 days, and Slack may permanently delete those more than a year old, so keep records you need somewhere else. The Pro plan costs $7.25 per user a month when you pay yearly.
- **Microsoft Teams**: Team chat and video calls, part of Microsoft 365. Microsoft's free Teams for home allows group meetings up to 60 minutes with up to 100 people. Teams for home is a personal product, not a business plan. Teams Essentials costs $4 per user a month paid yearly.
- **Google Chat**: Team chat, included in Google Workspace at no extra cost. Business Starter costs $7 per user a month on a yearly plan, or $8.40 on a flexible monthly plan.
- **Trello**: Tasks shown as cards on a board, with one column for each stage. The free plan allows up to 10 collaborators per workspace. The Standard plan costs $5 per user a month billed yearly, or $6 billed monthly.
- **Asana**: Task lists and projects for teams. The free Personal plan allows up to 2 users. The Starter plan costs $10.99 per user a month billed yearly, or $13.49 billed monthly.

Prices as of November 2026.

## What to watch for

- **One more app.** A team uses a new app only if it's simpler than the group chat they have. Start with one channel or one board and add more later.
- **Price per user.** Most of these apps charge for each person, so the cost grows with the team.
- **Phones for staff.** Decide whether staff use their own phones for work apps, and remove them from the workspace when they leave.
