## What it is

A website builder is a service where you make a website by picking a template and editing text and photos in your browser. Hosting means the computers that store your site and send it to visitors. With a website builder, the monthly price includes the hosting, so you don't rent it separately.

## When you need it

- You don't have a website, and people search for your business name and find only a map pin or an old social media page.
- You have a website, but you can't change your prices, hours, or photos without asking someone.
- Visitors read your site but don't call, book, or buy, because the site doesn't show a clear next step.
- Your site looks broken on a phone.

If most customers already find you through Instagram or Google Maps, a one-page site with your services, prices, and a way to contact you may be all you need.

## Popular choices

- **Squarespace**: Polished templates for a studio, restaurant, or portfolio where photos matter.
- **Wix**: Many templates and add-ons, with a drag-and-drop editor. It fits an owner who wants to place everything by hand.
- **Shopify**: Built for selling products online. It fits a shop more than a service business.
- **WordPress.com**: The hosted version of WordPress, the software behind many websites.

## What to watch for

- **Moving away later.** Most builders don't let you take your design to another service. You can usually copy your text and photos, but you rebuild the pages.
- **Add-ons that charge each month.** Booking, forms, and online stores often cost extra on top of the plan.
- **Who owns the domain.** Register your domain (your web address) in an account you control, so you keep it if you change builders or designers.
- **Speed on phones.** Large photos and many add-ons make pages slow on a phone. Open your site on your own phone over mobile data and check.
