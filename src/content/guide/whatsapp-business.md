## What it is

WhatsApp Business is a separate free app from Meta, the company that owns WhatsApp. It adds tools that regular WhatsApp doesn't have: a business profile with your address and hours, a catalog of products or services, saved replies you can send with a shortcut, an automatic away message, and labels to sort chats, such as "new order" or "paid".

For larger teams, Meta also sells the WhatsApp Business Platform. Software companies build on it to connect WhatsApp to other apps, so several people can answer from one number.

## When you need it

- You use your personal WhatsApp number for the business, and personal and customer chats are mixed.
- You type the same prices and opening hours into each new chat.
- You track orders or bookings in WhatsApp chats and lose track of which ones are done.
- Customers message you after hours and don't know when you'll reply.

If several people need to answer the same number at the same time, look at an app built on the WhatsApp Business Platform instead.

## Popular choices

- **WhatsApp Business app**: Free to download, on one phone, plus linked devices such as a computer. It fits a business with one or two people answering.
- **WhatsApp Business Platform**: Meta's paid service for connecting WhatsApp to other software. You usually use it through another company's app.

## What to watch for

- **Moving your number.** You can move your existing number to the Business app, but read WhatsApp's instructions first and back up your chats.
- **Orders kept only in chats.** Labels make chats easier to sort, but a chat is not an order list. If you take many orders, copy them into a list or an order app every day.
- **Rules on messages you start.** The Business Platform has its own rules and prices for messages a business sends first. Read them before you plan reminders or promotions.
