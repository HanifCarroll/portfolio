## Intent

A 1080x1080 English LinkedIn launch video for the Product Usage Scoring & Routing
system, 35–45 seconds, fully autonomous. Audience is prospective B2B SaaS consulting
clients. The core argument: noisy product-usage and customer-intent signals can be
turned into an inspectable, weekly action queue — with every routing and scoring
policy decision kept visible and explainable, never hidden behind a black box.

Tone is consulting-grade and credible: light-mode, calm, professional, confidence
over hype. Keep the established light-mode, consulting-grade cobalt design direction
(cobalt accents on light canvas, restrained typography, data-forward).
